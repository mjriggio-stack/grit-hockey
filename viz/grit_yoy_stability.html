Key Finding

GRIT z is more stable year-over-year than points across every single season pair in the dataset. The average gap is +0.068 r in favor of GRIT. This means a player's physical contribution rank relative to their peers is a more persistent signal than their scoring rank. The metric captures something structural about how players play, not just what they produced in a given year.
