GRIT v3.1

Contested-Puck Contribution for the NHL · 2015-16 through 2025-26
GRIT is a position-adjusted, weighted composite that measures how much a player contributes to contested-puck situations — hits, blocks, takeaways, physical penalties, defensive-zone faceoff wins, crease goals, close-range shots, and more. The metric is not a "toughness stat." It identifies players who do contested-puck work regardless of role: top-line wingers who draw penalties, fourth-line centers who throw 250 hits, and stay-at-home defensemen who block shots all surface honestly against their own positional pool. Validated across nine NHL seasons (2020-21 excluded for COVID), with year-over-year repeatability of r = 0.873 — well above public benchmarks for Corsi/Fenwick (~0.65) and Points/60 (~0.55). v3.1 adds close-range shot attempts as a 15th weighted component; see the CHANGELOG for the full v3 → v3.1 diff. Methodology and source code are in this repository.
